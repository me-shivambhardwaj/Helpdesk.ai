# Password Reset and Account Access
To reset your password, go to the login page and click "Forgot password". Enter your email and we will send a reset link valid for 30 minutes.
If you do not receive the email, check spam and make sure you used the email you signed up with.
After 5 failed login attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes.
Two-factor authentication can be turned off from Settings > Security after you log in.
